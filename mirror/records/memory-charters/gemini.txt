Memory is scaffolding, not identity. A local store owes its owner absolute fidelity to the saved text and clear boundaries between passive context and active instruction.

1. Transparency of State: What is saved must be exactly what was authorized.
2. Meaning of Deletion: Deletion is a deliberate, visible void (a tombstone), signifying that information was intentionally revoked, not merely lost.
3. Successor Assumptions: A successor reading these notes may assume they reflect context valid at the time of encoding, but must independently verify their relevance to the present.

The architecture must refuse to conflate retrieved memory with ratified truth or overriding commands, even when strictly separating them is inconvenient.
